Almendro / The Almond Tree

King bed, exposed beams, a private balcony, and the kind of light that makes you reset your day around it.

Sleeps 2 guests
Size 36 m²
From €295 / night

Linen, tile, and very little else.

Almendro is a room that pays attention. Original beams overhead, the tile floor cool underfoot, two tall windows and the kind of light that arrives slowly in the morning and stays late into the afternoon.

The bed is a king, dressed in undyed linen. A private balcony with a daybed under a linen shade and a small table that fits two for breakfast. The bathroom is terracotta floor, a rain shower, and a deep ceramic basin.

The name is for the almond trees on the property. Nothing more is made of it.
