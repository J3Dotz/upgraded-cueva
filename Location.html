How to arrive

Three ways to the mountain.All of them quiet by the end.

However you come, the last fifteen minutes feel like the trip is just beginning. Olive groves, then a single-track road, then open sky.

I.

By carEn coche

The recommended way

From the AP-7 motorway, take exit 45 and follow signage to Cabanes. The final approach is a single-track mountain lane — slow, narrow, worth it. Free parking on the estate.

  • From Valencia1 h 15
  • From Barcelona3 hrs
  • From Madrid4 h 30
II.

By trainEn tren

Then a short taxi

Renfe high-speed lines run from Madrid, Barcelona and Valencia to Benicàssim. We arrange the final twenty-minute taxi through a local driver — let us know your train and we'll meet you at the station.

  • Nearest stationBenicàssim
  • To the lodge20 min taxi
  • From Valencia Estació1 h 10
III.

By airEn avión

Valencia or Castellón

Valencia Airport (VLC) has the widest schedule and is ninety minutes by car. Castellón Costa Azahar (CDT) is closer — forty minutes — with seasonal routes. Hire a car or we'll send a driver.

  • Valencia (VLC)90 min
  • Castellón (CDT)40 min
  • Barcelona (BCN)3 hrs
